## Description

Elevate Plate plans your week of meals around what matters to you: your budget, your diet and how much time you actually have to cook.

THREE WAYS TO PLAN
• Plan it yourself: pick your own recipes for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
• Mix it up: plan the meals you already have in mind, and Elevate Plate fills every empty slot to fit your budget.
• Plan it for me: set a weekly budget, household size, cooking time (15 minutes to no limit) and diet, and get a full week of meals in seconds.

SHOP SMARTER
• One combined shopping list for the whole week, scaled to your household.
• Items already in your pantry or fridge are taken off automatically.
• Estimated prices and a live budget meter, so you know your total before you get to the store.
• Share your list by message, email or notes, and check items off as you shop, even without signal.

EAT SAFELY
• Add allergies and dislikes once. Recipes that contain them are left out of your plans and flagged in your library.
• Diets supported: vegetarian, vegan and pescatarian.

COOK WHAT YOU HAVE
• "What can I make?" ranks recipes by how much you already have at home.
• Discover thousands more recipes and add them to your library in one tap.

PLUS
• Weekly shopping reminders.
• Works on the web too: sign in at elevate-plate.vercel.app with the same account.

Prices are estimates based on typical U.S. grocery prices (including U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics averages) and may differ from your store. Always check ingredient labels if you have a serious allergy.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.

For every data type below: **Used for: App Functionality** only. **Linked to the user's
identity: Yes.** **Used for tracking: No.**

| Category | Data type | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact Info | Email Address | Account sign-in |
| Contact Info | Name | Optional display name |
| Identifiers | User ID | Internal account ID |
| Health & Fitness | Health | Allergies and dietary restrictions the user enters, used only to filter recipes |
| User Content | Other User Content | Recipes, meal plans, pantry and shopping items, budget preferences |

Not collected: location, contacts, browsing history, purchases, financial info, usage
data for analytics, diagnostics, advertising data. The app has no ads and no tracking, so
answer **No** to tracking everywhere, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is needed.
