Common questions

How do I plan my week?

On Home, choose how you want to plan: I'll plan it myself (pick your own recipes), Mix it up (plan some meals and let the app fill the rest), or Plan it for me (enter your budget, household size, cooking time and diet, and get a full week).

Where do the prices come from?

Prices are estimates. About 20 staples use U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics national average prices; the rest are typical US supermarket prices. They aren't live prices from a specific store, so your total at the register may differ. You can enter your own price for anything you add to your shopping list.

How do allergies and diets work?

Add them in the Diet tab. Recipes containing them are left out of generated plans and meal ideas, and flagged in your library. Groups like "Dairy" or "Gluten" cover their common ingredients, but always check labels yourself: the app can't guarantee a recipe is safe for a serious allergy.

Can I share my shopping list?

Yes. On the Shopping tab, tap Share list to send it by message, email or notes.

I forgot my password

Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll help you get back in.

How do I delete my account?

Open the account menu (the circle with your initial, top right), choose Delete my account, and confirm with your password. Everything is deleted permanently. See our Privacy Policy.
