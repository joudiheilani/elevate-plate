Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Elevate Plate is a meal-planning app that helps you plan meals around your budget, diet and cooking time. This policy explains what information the app stores, why, and how you can delete it. In short: we only keep what's needed to run the app, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, an optional name, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash (scrypt), never in readable form.
  • What you add to the app: recipes, meal plans, pantry items, shopping-list items and prices, your weekly budget, household size, cooking-time and diet preferences, and the allergies and dislikes you enter.
  • Allergies and dietary information may count as health-related information. It is used only to filter and flag recipes for you, and is never shared or used for anything else.
  • On your device: the app keeps your sign-in session, a copy of your data so it works offline, your checked-off shopping items and reminder settings. These stay on your device.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information (such as IP address, time and requested page) to operate and secure the service.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No selling or renting of your information.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the app, and only for that purpose:

  • Vercel hosts the app and its server (United States).
  • Neon hosts the database where your account and data are stored (United States).

We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the account menu (the circle with your initial), choose Delete my account, and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account and all of your data from our database straight away. Copies held in our providers' backups are removed on their normal backup schedule. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Security

All connections use HTTPS encryption, passwords are hashed, and each account can only access its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Elevate Plate is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
